## About

I'm a final-year Ph.D. candidate at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en) and Université de Montréal, advised by Irina Rish, and have collaborated with Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun. I study why large neural networks learn the representations they do, and how architecture and optimization shape them. My work combines theory, mostly tensor decomposition and random matrix theory, with controlled experiments on real language models.

Current threads:

- **Optimization dynamics in pretraining:** how temporary interventions keep changing training long after they end ([preprint, 2026](assets/files/papers/muon-temporary-weight-decay.pdf)).
- **Expressivity of transformer MLPs:** what gated blocks like SwiGLU can represent that tied blocks like ReLU² cannot ([CP-MLPs, NeurIPS 2026](assets/files/papers/cp-mlps.pdf)).
- **Memory in sequence models:** delta-rule and linear-attention architectures, and KV-cache compression ([TT-Delta, COLM 2026 workshop](https://openreview.net/forum?id=bnreIEZxps)).
- **Representation geometry:** collapse and compression across layers, and their link to reasoning ([Seq-VCR, ICLR 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02344); [Layer by Layer, ICML 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02013)).
